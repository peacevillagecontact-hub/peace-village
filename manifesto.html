Manifesto for a Self-Sustaining Community

In a world dominated by fiat systems and societal isolation, it is imperative to reclaim our autonomy and humanity. Our current reliance on mass-produced food and centralized currencies has led to environmental degradation, economic slavery, and disconnection from one another.

Our Vision

We aim to create a self-sustaining, solar-powered community that thrives on collective effort and shared resources. Inspired by eco-communities and B.F. Skinner's "Walden Two", this village will:

  • Own Land Collectively: No private ownership; land is for the community.
  • Ensure Food Independence: Grow our own food and eliminate reliance on external systems.
  • Achieve Energy Autonomy: Solar-powered systems for sustainable living.
  • Foster Transparency: Use Bitcoin and smart contracts to prevent corruption and ensure transparency in community.
  • Community Owned Businesses: Establish and manage community-owned enterprises that generate income to expand the land, build solar power infrastructure and sustain the village, ensuring all profits benefit the collective.
  • Support Each Other: Build permanent relationships, grow families, and live free from isolation.

This is a living document that will grow and evolve with our community. We invite all who share these values to join us in building this dream.

Why Community?

The fiat-driven system isolates us and fosters dependency. Living in a self-sufficient community liberates us from this cycle, offering support, purpose, and freedom. Together, we produce food, share resources, and reject the slavery of consumerism.

By embracing this vision, we not only sustain ourselves but also create a legacy of freedom, resilience, and unity for future generations.
