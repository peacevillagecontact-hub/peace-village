Manifesto for a Self-Sustaining Community

In a world dominated by fiat systems and societal isolation, it is imperative to reclaim our autonomy and humanity. Our current reliance on mass-produced food and centralized currencies has led to environmental degradation, economic slavery, and disconnection from one another.

Our Vision

We aim to create a self-sustaining, solar-powered community that thrives on collective effort and shared resources. Inspired by eco-communities and B.F. Skinner's "Walden Two", this village will:

  • Own Land Collectively: No private ownership; land is for the community.
  • Ensure Food Independence: Grow our own food and eliminate reliance on external systems.
  • Achieve Energy Autonomy: Solar-powered systems for sustainable living.
  • Foster Transparency: Open, traceable community decision-making and finances.
