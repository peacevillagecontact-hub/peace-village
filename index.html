Our Core Values

🌱 Self-Sustaining

Powered by solar energy and sustainable practices, living in harmony with nature.

🏞️ Community-Owned Land

Collectively owned and managed for shared benefit, ensuring equitable control.

🏢 Community-Owned Businesses

Cooperative enterprises generating income to expand land and support growth.

☀️ Solar-Powered Independence

Reliance on renewable solar energy with battery storage for resilience.

🌾 Self-Sustaining Food Production

Permaculture-based farming and community greenhouses for year-round sustenance.

💧 Sustainable Water Management

Access to natural water sources with purification and rainwater harvesting systems.

♻️ Circular Economy

Zero-waste systems and composting for a sustainable closed-loop ecosystem.

🤝 Consensus Governance

No boss, no politicians, no prime ministers. Consensus with the community in circles.

₿ Transparent Finances

Open, traceable financial management so every contribution is accountable to the community.

♥ Built with love, light and peace for humanity
