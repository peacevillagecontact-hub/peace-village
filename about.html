About Rainbow Peace Village

Peace Village is born from the magic, love, and connection experienced at Rainbow Gatherings. We envision a permanent, self-sustaining community where we can live in harmony with nature and one another, carrying the Rainbow spirit throughout the year.

Our Core Principles

  • Self-Sustainability: Powered by renewable energy and sustainable practices, we aim to live in tune with the land.
  • Collective Ownership: Land belongs to everyone. We practice collective care for the earth and each other.
  • Transparent Resource Management: Community resources are managed with complete transparency, possibly utilizing Bitcoin & blockchain technology for total transparency, community ownership and accountability.
  • Community-Owned Enterprises: Businesses run by the community contribute to our sustainability and growth to generate income to expand our free land and welcome more family.
  • Shared Responsibilities: Everyone contributes their unique skills and passions to the community.
  • Holistic Education: We raise our children as a community, fostering love, compassion, and respect for nature.

Inspired by works like B.F. Skinner's "Walden Two" and successful eco-communities around the world, we believe in the power of intentional living to create positive change. Our village will be a living experiment in sustainable, compassionate, and joyful coexistence.

We recognize that this vision requires patience, love, and collective effort. It's a long-term project, but one that we believe is vital for creating a more harmonious way of life on our planet.

Join us in manifesting this dream. Together, we can create a place where the Rainbow spirit thrives every day, where we work in unity, raise families, live sustainably, and welcome all who share this vision of a brighter, more connected future.
